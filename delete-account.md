# NOXYQ — Account & Data Deletion

**Developer:** NOXYQ Labs  
**Application:** NOXYQ

## Request Account Deletion

NOXYQ respects your right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal information.

To request account deletion, please contact:

**noxyqlabs@gmail.com**

Use the subject:

**NOXYQ Account Deletion Request**

Please send the request from the email address associated with your NOXYQ account, if possible.

Do not include your password, authentication tokens, or other sensitive credentials in your message.

## What Happens Next?

After receiving your request, we may ask you to verify account ownership to prevent unauthorized deletion.

Once the request has been verified, we will process the deletion in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Account deletion may include the removal of:

- Your NOXYQ account profile and associated personal information.
- Authentication sessions and account-related access credentials.
- Personal application preferences stored in NOXYQ Cloud.
- Personal account-associated data that is no longer required.

## Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained for legitimate legal, tax, payment, fraud-prevention, or security obligations.

Organization-owned monitoring records and shared resources may require separate handling where they also belong to other authorized organization members.

Any retained information will be limited to the applicable purpose and retention requirements.

Locally stored information can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling NOXYQ.

Information already submitted to independent third-party measurement providers is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.
