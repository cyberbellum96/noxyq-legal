# NOXYQ — Account & Data Deletion

[English](./delete-account.html) | [Українська](./delete-account-uk.html)

**Developer:** NOXYQ Labs  
**Application:** NOXYQ  
**Last updated: October 18, 2026**

## Current Production Guest Free 1.0

The current Android release has no NOXYQ registration, sign-in, Cloud account or account-deletion API. You do not need an account email to delete Guest data. Account, Cloud, Android Agent, purchases and subscriptions are not active in this release.

## Delete Local Data

- Clear supported histories using each tool's controls. Speed Test and Wake-on-LAN histories retain up to 50 results each; this limit does not apply to every record or profile.
- Delete Wake-on-LAN profiles and schedules using the tool. Deleting a profile removes its stored SecureOn value and schedules. Clearing history alone does not delete profiles.
- Clearing Speed Test history retains provider consent. Clearing NOXYQ app storage in Android removes local settings, histories and consent records.
- Uninstalling removes local app data, subject to Android transfer/restore behavior. Android cloud backup is disabled; this does not guarantee that every device-to-device transfer is prevented.

## External Measurement Data

Local deletion does not delete records already received or published by CoverageMap, M-Lab or another diagnostic destination. M-Lab's public datasets are retained indefinitely; independent providers determine their own available deletion mechanisms. NOXYQ does not promise to delete those datasets. See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.html) and the provider policies linked there.

Contact **noxyqlabs@gmail.com** to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence we hold. Gmail receives voluntary support messages and associated metadata. Correspondence is kept while needed to address a request or meet applicable obligations. Requests concern the information we actually hold and applicable law.

Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or SecureOn values.

## Future Account-Enabled Versions

A future version enabling NOXYQ accounts would need its own applicable data controls and policy review before release. This page describes the current Guest release and does not announce an active account service or a commitment to deliver one.
