# Privacy Policy

**NOXYQ — Network Diagnostics & Monitoring**

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to NOXYQ.

NOXYQ is a network diagnostics, analysis, and monitoring platform developed under the name **NOXYQ Labs**, operated by an independent developer based in Ukraine.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information NOXYQ may access, collect, process, store, and share when you use our Android application and connected services.

By using NOXYQ, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

**Contact:** noxyqlabs@gmail.com

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on which features you use, NOXYQ may process the following categories of information.

### 2.1 Network and Device Information

To provide network diagnostics, NOXYQ may access:

- Local and public IP addresses.
- Wi-Fi network names (SSID) and access point identifiers (BSSID).
- MAC addresses of discoverable network devices, when available.
- Network gateway and DNS information.
- Wi-Fi signal strength, frequencies, and channel information.
- Information about local network devices and available services.
- Network connection type and technical connection characteristics.
- Network latency, packet loss, download speed, and upload speed.
- Hostnames, network ports, and diagnostic responses.

This information is processed to perform the network analysis or diagnostic operation requested by the user.

Most local network diagnostic operations are performed directly on your device.

Local network scanning results are not automatically uploaded to your NOXYQ account. Where Cloud synchronization is supported, network inventory data is transferred only after the user initiates or confirms the relevant synchronization.

### 2.2 Account Information

NOXYQ can be used in Guest Mode for supported local network tools without creating an account.

If you register or sign in to a NOXYQ account, we may process:

- Email address.
- Account identifiers.
- Authentication and session information.
- Account preferences and settings.
- Organization membership and permissions.
- Information necessary to manage subscriptions and access rights.

Account information is used to provide authentication, synchronize supported preferences, secure account access, and enable Cloud-connected services.

Authentication credentials are handled using security controls appropriate to their purpose.

### 2.3 Technical and Diagnostic Information

When you interact with NOXYQ services, certain technical information may be processed, including:

- Connection timestamps.
- IP addresses used to communicate with NOXYQ servers.
- Application and API request information.
- Technical errors and service diagnostics.
- Session and installation identifiers.
- Security and authentication events.

This information helps us operate the service, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and maintain security.

### 2.4 Locally Stored Information

NOXYQ may store the following information on your device:

- Application settings and preferences.
- Local diagnostic history.
- Saved diagnostic configurations.
- Cached network information.
- Session-related information required for supported functionality.

Local application data is generally controlled through the application and Android operating system.

Uninstalling NOXYQ or clearing application storage may remove locally stored data. Information previously synchronized with NOXYQ Cloud may remain associated with your account.

## 3. Device Permissions

NOXYQ requests Android permissions only when they are required for supported application functionality.

Depending on your Android version and selected features, these permissions may include:

**Network and Internet Access**

Used to perform internet connectivity tests, communicate with network devices, and access Cloud services.

**Wi-Fi and Nearby Devices**

Used to discover nearby Wi-Fi networks, inspect connection properties, and identify devices or services on the local network.

**Location Permission**

Certain Android versions require location-related permissions to access Wi-Fi scan information, including nearby access point identifiers.

NOXYQ uses such access for network diagnostics. Location-related permission does not, by itself, mean that NOXYQ continuously tracks or uploads your geographic location.

**Notifications**

Where supported and enabled, notifications may be used to communicate application or service status.

Permissions can be managed through Android system settings. Denying a permission may limit the functionality that depends on it.

We do not request unrelated sensitive permissions solely for advertising purposes.

## 4. How We Use Information

We process information for the following purposes:

- Providing network diagnostic and monitoring features.
- Displaying Wi-Fi and network connection information.
- Performing user-requested tests and scans.
- Saving diagnostic results and application settings.
- Authenticating users and protecting accounts.
- Synchronizing supported information with NOXYQ Cloud.
- Managing organizations, permissions, and subscriptions.
- Maintaining service reliability and security.
- Diagnosing technical problems.
- Complying with applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable, processing is based on providing requested services, legitimate security and operational interests, consent, or legal requirements.

We do not sell personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

## 5. Network Diagnostics and Third-Party Services

Some NOXYQ features communicate with external servers to perform the diagnostic operation selected by the user.

For example:

- Ping and Traceroute send network probes to selected destinations.
- DNS tools communicate with DNS infrastructure.
- Speed Test communicates with selected measurement servers.
- Network discovery communicates with devices and services on the local network.

When a diagnostic connection is established, the destination server or service may receive technical connection information, including the source public IP address.

For supported internet speed measurements, NOXYQ may use external measurement infrastructure, including **Measurement Lab (M-Lab)** or **CoverageMap**, depending on the selected provider and service availability.

These providers may process measurement information according to their own policies.

Users should review applicable third-party privacy practices when using external services.

NOXYQ does not control the independent data processing practices of external diagnostic destinations.

**Important information about Speed Test providers**

When a user initiates a speed test, NOXYQ may transmit network measurements and related technical information to the selected external measurement provider.

CoverageMap may receive and retain completed test results, including measurement information, device metadata, and application information, in accordance with its privacy policy.

Measurement Lab (M-Lab) may collect and publicly publish technical measurement data, including the public IP address used during the test. M-Lab retains published datasets indefinitely and may not be able to delete individual test records.

Users should review the privacy policies of these providers before starting a measurement:

- CoverageMap: https://coveragemap.com/privacy-policy
- M-Lab: https://www.measurementlab.net/privacy/

## 6. Cloud Synchronization

Certain NOXYQ features may connect to NOXYQ Cloud when the user signs in or enables supported Cloud functionality.

Cloud-connected data may include:

- Account and organization details.
- User preferences.
- Registered agents and monitoring targets.
- Monitoring observations and incident history.
- Network inventory information explicitly synchronized by the user.
- Information required for subscription and licensing management.

Cloud data is associated with the relevant account or organization and is subject to applicable access permissions.

Using local network tools in Guest Mode does not automatically create a NOXYQ Cloud account or upload a complete history of local network scans.

## 7. Payments and Subscriptions

NOXYQ may offer optional paid features or subscriptions.

When purchases are made through Google Play, payment processing is managed by Google Play and its payment services.

NOXYQ may receive subscription-related information necessary to verify purchases and manage access, such as:

- Purchase or subscription identifiers.
- Purchased product information.
- Subscription status.
- Verification and expiration information.

NOXYQ does not require users to provide their complete payment card details directly to the application for Google Play purchases.

Payment-related information is handled in accordance with applicable payment provider policies.

## 8. Information Sharing

We may share or disclose information only when necessary to:

- Provide a feature or service requested by the user.
- Communicate with selected network diagnostic destinations.
- Operate infrastructure through authorized service providers.
- Verify purchases and subscriptions.
- Comply with applicable laws and lawful requests.
- Protect users, our services, and network infrastructure from fraud or abuse.

Service providers may process information on our behalf under applicable contractual and security requirements.

We do not sell collected network information for advertising purposes.

## 9. Data Storage and Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, fulfill legitimate operational or security requirements, or comply with applicable laws.

Local data may be removed by clearing application storage or uninstalling NOXYQ.

Account and Cloud information may remain stored while an account or its associated organization is active.

Different types of Cloud data may have different retention periods. Subscription-based history access limits do not necessarily represent the physical deletion date of stored records.

When information is no longer required, we take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize it, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

## 10. Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

These measures may include:

- Authenticated access to Cloud services.
- Access controls and organization-level permissions.
- Secure handling of authentication credentials.

- Restrictions on access to sensitive application data.
- Security monitoring and abuse prevention.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.
